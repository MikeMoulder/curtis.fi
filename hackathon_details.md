# BOT Chain Builder Challenge #2

## AI × RWA Builder Challenge

BOT Chain Builder Challenge #2 focuses on two core tracks — **AI** and **RWA (Real World Assets)** — encouraging developers to build on-chain applications that deliver real user value, not just complete smart contract deployments.

**This Challenge places greater emphasis on product completeness, real-world usability, and Mainnet deployment**, prioritizing support for high-quality projects that can sustain long-term contribution to the BOT Chain ecosystem.

🏆 **Total Prize Pool: Up to 5,000 USDT**

Final winning projects and prize allocation will be determined based on overall project quality and review results.

---

## 👥 Who Should Participate

This Challenge welcomes the following developers and teams:

- AI application developers
- RWA (Real World Assets) development teams
- Web3 developers
- Solidity / smart contract developers
- Full-stack developers
- Startup teams
- University developers and student teams
- Mature project teams already live on other EVM chains looking to expand to BOT Chain

Whether you're building from scratch or looking to deploy an existing product to BOT Chain, all are welcome to register as long as the activity requirements are met.

## 🚀 Challenge Tracks

| Track | Project Directions (including but not limited to) |
| --- | --- |
| 🏆 RWA Applications (Real World Asset Applications) | Real asset tokenization, asset management, asset distribution, RWA restaking, product aggregation, RWA data services, compliance tools, infrastructure, etc. |
| 🤖 AI Native Applications | AI Agents, AI-driven on-chain automation, AI-powered trading and decision-making, AI asset issuance, AI data and identity, AI infrastructure, etc. |

BOT Chain Builder Challenge #2 focuses on two core tracks: AI and RWA.

**RWA is BOT Chain's current key ecosystem development direction**, with a dedicated review dimension focused on real asset applications, asset management, restaking, product aggregation, and related infrastructure.

**The AI track will be evaluated according to independent track standards**, focusing on whether AI serves as a core product capability participating in on-chain business processes, asset issuance, or automated decision-making — it will not be directly compared against RWA projects.

---

## 📌 Track Definitions

### AI Native Applications

AI should serve as the core capability or on-chain decision-making entity of the product, capable of participating in on-chain asset issuance, trading, automated execution, or business processes — rather than merely serving as auxiliary copywriting generation, simple chat functionality, or a third-party AI API call.

### RWA Projects

Projects should be built around real-world asset ecosystems, including but not limited to asset issuance, asset management, asset distribution, restaking, product aggregation, revenue distribution, data services, and infrastructure — and should clearly articulate their business logic and value loop.

## 🌳 Migration Projects

Mature projects already live on other chains are welcome to deploy to BOT Chain. To ensure genuine ecosystem integration, migration projects must meet the following requirements:

- Complete core contract deployment on BOT Chain
- Complete frontend network support
- Complete wallet interaction adaptation
- Establish the core business flow

**Simply copying contracts or completing a superficial deployment will not be considered as meeting participation requirements.**

Additionally, please provide the following information:

- Why did you choose BOT Chain?
- What new capabilities does the BOT Chain version add?
- How will you continue to acquire users and increase on-chain activity going forward?

BOT Chain encourages excellent projects to migrate to the ecosystem, but places greater emphasis on whether the project will continue to iterate based on BOT Chain, rather than a one-time deployment.

## ⭐ Priority Projects

The following types of projects will receive additional attention during review:

- **RWA (Real World Assets) related applications (highest priority):** Priority given to applications centered around asset management, asset distribution, restaking, product aggregation, and related infrastructure.
- **AI Native Applications:** AI should serve as a core product capability and be able to drive on-chain business processes or asset-related operations, rather than merely offering auxiliary AI functions.
- Mature projects already live on other chains that have completed substantive integration with BOT Chain
- Applications with real user use cases and commercial value
- Projects with an ongoing maintenance plan and long-term development roadmap

The total prize pool for this Challenge is up to **5,000 USDT**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. Who can join?**
BOT Chain Builder Challenge #2 is open to developers worldwide, including AI & RWA developers, Solidity / EVM developers, full-stack developers, startup teams, university builders, and existing EVM projects looking to expand to BOT Chain.
Both individuals and teams are welcome.

**2. Does my project have to be brand new?**
No. Existing projects are welcome, provided they complete meaningful integration with BOT Chain, including Mainnet deployment, frontend network support, wallet integration, and core user workflows.
Simple contract migration or formal deployment alone does not qualify.

**3. Can I migrate an existing project from another blockchain?**
Yes. If your project has already launched on another blockchain, please additionally explain:

- Why did you choose BOT Chain?
- What new capabilities are introduced in the BOT Chain version?
- How will you continue to grow users and on-chain activity?

**4. Does my project have to be deployed on BOT Chain Mainnet?**
Yes. Only projects successfully deployed on BOT Chain Mainnet are eligible for final judging.
Projects deployed only on Testnet will not enter the final evaluation.

**5. What are the minimum submission requirements?**
Your submission should include:

- BOT Chain Mainnet deployment
- Public website or online demo
- Wallet integration
- GitHub repository
- A complete user or business workflow

Please refer to the Full Challenge Details & Rules for complete submission requirements.

**6. Does my project have to be open source?**
A GitHub repository is required for technical review.
he repository does not need to be publicly accessible, but the judging team must be granted sufficient access to review the submitted implementation during the evaluation period.

**7. Can I use AI tools during development?**
Absolutely. We encourage developers to use AI tools throughout the development process.
However, projects submitted under the AI Native Applications track should use AI as a core capability, rather than simply integrating third-party AI APIs or chatbot features.

**8. How are winners selected?**
Projects will be evaluated based on:

- Product quality and completeness
- Technical implementation
- Innovation
- User value
- BOT Chain ecosystem fit

RWA Applications and AI Native Applications will be reviewed under their respective track criteria.

**9. How are prizes paid?**
Prizes will be distributed in USDT after the winners are announced and wallet information has been verified.

**10. Where can I ask questions?**
Join the BOT Chain Builder Hub: https://t.me/BotChain_official/61
Official announcements, technical support, event updates, and submission reminders will be shared in the Builder Hub throughout the Challenge.

**11. My project wasn't selected. Can I still receive ecosystem support?**
Yes. Not being selected in this Challenge does not necessarily mean your project lacks value. Outstanding projects may still be considered for future ecosystem collaboration, incubation, grants, or other BOT Chain Builder Programs.
We encourage all builders to continue improving their projects and participate in future BOT Chain Developer Activities.
